# RIVALS lobby and gameplay visual redesign

Mode: Operate. User approved mockup B and explicitly asked to extend it into
the game. Preserve AI, online rooms, loadout and configurable touch controls.
Approved reference: .impeccable/mocks/menu-b.png. Seed: 8eaea888.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A competitive broadcast lobby that transitions into a readable arena
HUD, replacing disconnected neon panels with one visual grammar.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight/navy surfaces, cold white text, cobalt selections and signal
yellow primary actions. Condensed athletic display, neutral readable body type,
fine rules and purposeful rectangles. No decorative glows or gradient lettering.

STORY: See the arena, choose AI or an online duel, choose difficulty, play.
In-match, immediately read health, score, ammunition and weapon state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile portrait has a compact RIVALS masthead, arena image across
roughly 40% of the view, and a lower match sheet with mode tabs, difficulty row,
yellow play button, online shortcut, armory and footer controls. Desktop places
the scene beside a bounded match sheet. Settings open in a focused panel.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3, competitive broadcast match desk, seed 8eaea888.
Composition B selected by the user. Signature interaction: selected mode moves
the blue emphasis between AI and Online without displacing the arena preview.
Gameplay uses the same palette with concrete/cobalt/coral cover and sunset light;
existing colliders and visibility-critical aiming space remain intact.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
